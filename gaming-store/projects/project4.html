Apex Fitness

Campaign-led design system across print and digital assets — motivating performance through bold visual language.

Category
Marketing · Print
Client
Apex Fitness Group
Year
2024

The Challenge

Apex Fitness was launching three new club locations simultaneously and needed a campaign system that could scale across OOH, print collateral, social, and in-club environments — all under a tight 6-week timeline and a shared visual system.

The brief called for high-energy creative that felt premium rather than aggressive — an aesthetic that would appeal to their growing female and over-35 demographic without losing the performance credibility existing members expected.

The Solution

We developed a campaign around the theme of "Precision" — controlled power rather than raw intensity. The typographic system uses compressed grotesque headlines in a mix of weights to create dynamic hierarchy. Photography direction used clean studio lighting against rich dark backdrops to balance energy with elegance.

The modular grid system allowed every asset — from 6-sheet billboards to A5 leaflets — to be produced efficiently without losing visual cohesion across formats.
