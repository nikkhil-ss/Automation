Packaging

Kova Coffee

Packaging visuals and shelf branding for specialty coffee SKUs — a sensory identity that earns its place on the counter.

Category
Packaging
Client
Kova Specialty Coffee
Year
2024

The Challenge

Kova had built a loyal following at their single café but needed packaging strong enough to carry the brand into wholesale and retail channels. The identity needed to communicate specialist knowledge and provenance — competing against established names on supermarket shelves — without losing the warmth of an independent roaster.

The Solution

We built around a concept of "origin storytelling" — each SKU carries detailed provenance information structured as part of the visual design rather than an afterthought. A distinctive die-cut window on the front of each bag lets the roast quality speak for itself while the surrounding graphic language frames it with editorial intention.

Colour coding per origin region allows easy navigation at shelf. The brand uses a restrained hand-drawn logotype set against structured typographic grids — approachable but considered.
